Due to the nature of this Resource Pack, 
there are some settings I would recommended 
to get the best experience.

All settings not mentioned here can 
be whatever you would like.

Some options are only recommended, 
but others can majorly effect the quality, 
and will thus be labeled as "required".
(Technically not required, but very much 
recommended other the other stuff.)

<Video>
Rendering:	Pixelated	(recommended)
Scaling:	Integer		(recommended)
Drop Shadows:	Off		(recommended)

<Audio>
Menu BGM:	Off		(recommended, I have not replaced this music yet)

<Visuals>
Parallax:	None		(recommended, Backgrounds were made with no parallax in mind)
BG Particles:	Off		(required, these use base game sprites and look super out of place)
HUD Style:	Modern		(required, this classic HUD style does not work with this pack)
Rainbow Effect:	Classic		(required, modern looks very out of place)
Text Shadows:	Off		(required, the shadows do now work well with how I have the text set up)
Extra Effects:	Off		(required, these use base game sprites and look super out of place)
